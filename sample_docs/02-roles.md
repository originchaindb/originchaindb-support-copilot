# BeaconDesk: member roles

BeaconDesk has three workspace roles: owner, administrator, and member.

Owners manage billing, transfer ownership, and delete the workspace.
Administrators can invite members and manage projects, but cannot manage
billing or transfer ownership. Members can create and edit tasks in the
projects they can access.

To change a member's role, an owner opens Settings > Members, chooses the
member, and selects Change role. There must always be at least one owner.
An administrator cannot promote themselves to owner.

A project can be private. Only invited project members and workspace owners
can open a private project. Adding someone to a workspace does not
automatically add them to every private project.
