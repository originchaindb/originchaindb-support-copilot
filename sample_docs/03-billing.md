# BeaconDesk: billing and cancellation

These are fictional example policies, not OriginChainDB pricing or terms.

Workspace owners can update the payment method in Settings > Billing.
Invoices are available in Settings > Billing > Invoice history.
Administrators and members cannot download invoices.

To cancel a subscription, the workspace owner opens Settings > Billing and
selects Cancel subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the
current billing period. The workspace remains accessible until that date.

The trial lasts fourteen days. Trial workspaces support three members and
five projects. No payment method is required to start a trial.

This document does not specify refunds, discounts, taxes, or annual pricing.
Questions about those topics require a human response.
