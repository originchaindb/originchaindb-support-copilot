# BeaconDesk: workspaces and invitations

BeaconDesk is a fictional SaaS product used only in this tutorial.

## Create a workspace

After signing in, select Create workspace, enter a name, and choose Create.
The person creating a workspace becomes its owner. Workspace names can be
changed later from Settings > General. Changing a name does not change the
workspace identifier.

## Invite a teammate

To invite a teammate, open Settings > Members, select Invite member, enter their
email address, and choose a role. Invitations expire after seven days.
If an invitation expires, an owner or administrator can send a new invitation
from the Members page. An invitation does not become an active member until
the recipient accepts it.

## Leave a workspace

Members can leave from Settings > Profile > Leave workspace. The only remaining
owner must transfer ownership before leaving. Leaving a workspace removes
access to its projects but does not delete the workspace.
