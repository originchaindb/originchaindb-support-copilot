# BeaconDesk: notifications

Each member controls their own notifications in Settings > Notifications.
Email notifications can be enabled for task assignments, mentions, and
upcoming due dates. Turning off email notifications does not remove
notifications from the in-app inbox.

Quiet hours pause email notifications during a chosen daily time window.
Quiet hours use the time zone selected in Settings > Profile.
The in-app inbox continues to receive notifications during quiet hours.

Daily digests summarize changes to followed projects and arrive at 09:00
in the member's selected time zone. Members can switch between immediate
email notifications and a daily digest from the Notifications page.
